# Knowledge Content Roadmap — First Publishing Wave

**Status:** Proposed topics only. **No article has been written.**
**Scope:** 21 candidate topics across the seven categories, 3 per category.

Every topic below is a **candidate**, not a commitment. Each must pass all five gates in
`EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md` §7 before it is written, and any topic that cannot be written
without crossing the YMYL boundary is dropped rather than softened.

---

## Sequencing principle

**Wave 1 is one article per category — seven articles.** That seeds all seven clusters
evenly and makes each category page real rather than promissory.

**Wave 2 takes the three highest-intent categories to three articles each**, which
activates the Related Resources slot on those service pages (`INTERNAL_LINKING.md` §4).

Priority order for depth: **Patient Care → Elder Care → Mother & Newborn Care**. These
are the highest-intent, highest-anxiety services, and the ones where a family is most
likely to be searching at the moment they need to hire.

**Cadence is deliberately unstated.** No date is published, and no article ships to meet
a schedule. Skipping a month beats publishing filler.

## Deliberately excluded from Wave 1

| Excluded | Reason |
|---|---|
| Kota-specific local guides | Would need verified hospital names, discharge timings and equipment-hire options. Each fact needs checking before publication, and stale local facts are the most damaging kind. Revisit once the seven core clusters are seeded |
| Any pricing or cost article | The business does not publish fixed prices. An article about cost that cannot state cost is filler |
| "Ultimate guide" or hub-style mega-articles | Would compete with the category page for the same query |
| Comparison against named competitors | Not the business's story to tell |
| Anything requiring a medical claim to be useful | If the boundary removes the substance, the topic was never ours |

## Before writing anything

The highest-return item is still **not** an article: the Google Business Profile does not
yet exist. A verified GBP with real photos, correct NAP and active Posts will outperform
this entire roadmap for local intent, and it is unblocked today.

Write the articles because they are genuinely useful to families. Do not expect them to
substitute for the GBP.
