What's included

What Child Care Includes

Child Care is attentive, in-home supervision for young children while parents are at work or away. It is safe, engaged care and daily support — not teaching, and not medical treatment.

Care and supervision

  • Safe, attentive supervision through the day
  • Age-appropriate play and activities to keep children engaged
  • Meals and snacks served on time, with help eating where needed
  • Help with naps, hygiene and daily routines

Keeping to your routine

  • Following the routine and rules you already use at home
  • Getting children ready for school and settled again afterwards
  • Keeping their space clean, safe and tidy
  • Telling you honestly how the day went and what you should know
  • Relief for a parent juggling work and young children alone

What this service does not cover

  • This is not medical or nursing care, and we do not give medicines beyond a routine you have already set
  • We do not run formal lessons, tutoring or schooling
  • For a days-old baby and a recovering mother, the right service is Mother & Newborn Care
  • Full household work is arranged separately, not folded into child care duty

Why we state this plainly

Knowing where our work stops means nobody discovers a gap on the first day. If what your child needs sits outside this — medical attention, or the newborn stage — we will say so on the call and point you to the right service, including when that is not one of ours.

What to expect

How Arranging Child Care Works

Five stages, told from your side, so you know what you are agreeing to before you agree to it.

1 · First contact

Call or message and tell us the basics — your children's ages, where you are in Kota, and the hours you need covered.

2 · We listen first

We ask about your child's routine, what a normal day looks like, and any particular needs — then tell you what kind of arrangement actually fits.

3 · Matching the right person

We suggest verified caregivers experienced with children of that age and share their details. You decide who you are comfortable leaving your child with.

4 · Scheduling and start

We agree the hours, make expectations clear on both sides, and the caregiver starts — usually with a short settling-in period so your child gets used to them.

5 · Ongoing support

We stay reachable while the service runs. As your child grows or your hours change, tell us and we will adjust the arrangement.

Transparency

How Pricing Works

We do not publish rates, because a number set before we know your family would not be an honest one. What we can do is tell you exactly what the charge depends on.

  • The scope of care — how many children, their ages, and how much hands-on attention they need.
  • Duration — hours per day, days per week, and how long the arrangement is expected to run.
  • Pattern of hours — part-day, school hours, a full working day, or longer where a household needs it.
  • Specific requirements — what your household and your child's routine actually ask of the person doing the work.

We discuss the exact charge with you once we understand the situation, and there is no obligation to go ahead after that conversation.

Common questions

Child Care — Common Questions

The things families ask us on the phone, answered here so you do not have to.

What is the difference between Child Care and Mother & Newborn Care?

Child Care is for young children — supervision, play, meals and daily routines while parents are at work or away. Mother & Newborn Care is for the first weeks after delivery, supporting the mother's recovery and the newborn together. If your baby is very new and the mother is still recovering, that is Mother & Newborn Care; for an older child who needs looking after during the day, this is the service that fits.

What does the caregiver do with the children through the day?

Supervision and safety first — keeping your child safe, occupied and settled. Alongside that: age-appropriate play and activities, meals and snacks served on time, help with naps and daily routines, and keeping their space clean and tidy. We keep to the routine you already use at home rather than imposing a new one.

What hours can you arrange — school hours, a full day, or longer?

We arrange flexible hours to fit how your day runs — a few hours, school-day cover, after-school, a full working day, or longer where a household needs it. Tell us the pattern you need and we will tell you honestly what we can arrange, rather than promise something we cannot hold.

Is this teaching, tutoring, or medical care?

No. This is attentive care and supervision, not formal teaching or tutoring, and not medical or nursing care. We do not run lessons, and we do not diagnose or give medicines beyond a routine a parent has already set and explained. If a child needs medical attention, that belongs with your doctor.

How do you make sure my child is safe with your caregivers?

Every caregiver goes through a verification process before placement — identity verification, background checks, and an assessment of their experience and suitability for looking after children. We share the relevant details with you before the service begins, we match on temperament as well as experience, and if the person is not the right fit for your child, tell us and we will arrange someone else.

How are child care charges decided?

Charges depend on four things: the number and ages of the children, how many hours per day and days per week, the pattern of hours (part-day, full day, or longer), and any specific requirements of your household. We discuss the exact charge with you after we understand the situation, with no obligation to proceed. We do not publish rates on this page because a number set without knowing your family would not be an honest one.
