What's included

What Housekeeping / Dusting & Cleaning Includes

Housekeeping is a cleaning-focused service — keeping your home thoroughly clean and in order. We agree the rooms and the task list with you before the work starts, so everyone is clear from the first day.

Cleaning and dusting

  • Dusting throughout the home
  • Sweeping and mopping floors
  • Wiping and cleaning surfaces
  • Keeping rooms clean and in order

Bathrooms, kitchen and upkeep

  • Cleaning bathrooms
  • Cleaning the kitchen area
  • General cleanliness and daily upkeep
  • Keeping the home tidy alongside the cleaning

What this service does not cover

  • Cooking and meal preparation are not part of the service — that is Home Cook Services
  • General everyday household help is not the focus — that is Maid Services
  • Laundry is not a core part of housekeeping
  • We do not advertise deep cleaning, sanitisation, or commercial or industrial cleaning

Why we state this plainly

Knowing where our work stops means nobody discovers a gap on the first day. This is regular, thorough cleaning — if you need cooking, everyday household help, or something beyond regular cleaning, we will say so and point you to the right service rather than overpromise.

What to expect

How Arranging Housekeeping Works

Five stages, told from your side, so you know what you are agreeing to before you agree to it.

1 · First contact

Call or message and tell us the basics — where you are in Kota, the size of your home, and how often you would like it cleaned.

2 · We listen first

We ask which rooms matter most, how thorough you want the cleaning, and how many days a week — then tell you what kind of arrangement fits.

3 · Matching the right person

We suggest verified staff whose experience fits the work and share their details. You decide who you are comfortable having in your home.

4 · Scheduling and start

We agree the rooms, the schedule and the task list, make expectations clear on both sides, and the housekeeper starts.

5 · Ongoing support

We stay reachable while the service runs. If the rooms, the schedule or the fit need to change, tell us and we will sort it out.

Transparency

How Pricing Works

We do not publish rates, because a number set before we know your home would not be an honest one. What we can do is tell you exactly what the charge depends on.

  • The size of the home — the number of rooms and bathrooms to be cleaned.
  • Duration — how many days per week, and how long the arrangement is expected to run.
  • How thorough — a regular clean or a more detailed one, and the timings you need.
  • Specific requirements — the rooms that matter most and anything particular to your home.

We discuss the exact charge with you once we understand what you need, and there is no obligation to go ahead after that conversation.

Common questions

Housekeeping / Dusting & Cleaning — Common Questions

The things households ask us on the phone, answered here so you do not have to.

What does housekeeping cover?

Thorough cleaning of the home: dusting, sweeping and mopping floors, wiping surfaces, cleaning bathrooms, and cleaning the kitchen area — keeping the house clean and in order. We agree the rooms and the task list with you before the work starts, so it is clear what is included.

What is the difference between Housekeeping and Maid Services?

Housekeeping / Dusting & Cleaning is focused on cleaning as the main job — done thoroughly, room by room. Maid Services is broader everyday household help — a bit of everything, including utensils, laundry and daily chores alongside light cleaning. If cleaning is really what you want done well, choose Housekeeping; if you want general daily help, Maid Services fits better.

Do you offer deep cleaning or sanitisation?

We provide thorough regular cleaning — dusting, floors, surfaces, bathrooms and the kitchen area. We do not advertise deep cleaning, sanitisation, or commercial or industrial cleaning as separate services, and we are not a pest-control or restoration service. If your home needs something beyond regular cleaning, tell us and we will be honest about whether it is something we can arrange.

Does the housekeeper cook or do laundry?

No — cleaning is the job. Cooking and meal preparation are not part of housekeeping (that is Home Cook Services), and laundry is not a core responsibility here. If you need those alongside the cleaning, we can arrange Home Cook Services or Maid Services separately.

Are your housekeeping staff verified?

Yes. All staff go through a verification process before placement, including identity verification, background checks, and an assessment of their experience and suitability for the work. We share the relevant details with you before the service begins, because they will be working inside your home.

How are housekeeping charges decided?

Charges depend on the size of the home, the number of rooms and bathrooms, how thorough the cleaning needs to be, and how many days per week you need. We discuss the exact charge with you after we understand what you need, with no obligation to proceed. We do not publish rates on this page because a number set without knowing your home would not be an honest one.
