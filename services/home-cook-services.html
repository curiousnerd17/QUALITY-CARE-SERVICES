What's included

What Home Cook Services Includes

Home Cook Services is a dedicated cook for your household — fresh meals prepared daily to your family's taste. We agree the meals, timings and preferences with you before the cook starts, so everyone is clear from the first day.

Fresh daily cooking

  • Fresh, home-style meals prepared daily
  • Cooking to your family's taste and usual menu
  • Breakfast, lunch, dinner, or the combination you need
  • Keeping the cooking area tidy while they work

Cooking to your preferences

  • Vegetarian or non-vegetarian, as your household prefers
  • Regional cuisines and everyday home cooking
  • Less oil, less spice, or other preferences you set
  • Following a dietary instruction a family member has been given

What this service does not cover

  • General household chores are not part of the service — that is Maid Services
  • Cleaning, dusting and routine housekeeping are not included — that is Housekeeping / Dusting & Cleaning
  • Laundry is not part of a cook's duty
  • This is a home cook, not a commercial kitchen or catering service

Why we state this plainly

Knowing where our work stops means nobody discovers a gap on the first day. A cook is here to cook — if you also need the house cleaned or the chores done, we will say so and arrange Maid Services or Housekeeping, rather than stretch one person across everything.

Who it's for

Who Home Cook Services Is For

Most households who call us about a home cook are in one of these situations.

A working household with no time to cook

Everyone gets home late, and cooking fresh every day is the thing that keeps slipping. You want proper home food without the daily effort.

A family that wants fresh home food daily

You would rather have meals cooked fresh at home, to your own taste, than rely on outside food or reheated leftovers.

Elderly parents who need regular meals

An older couple or parent who should be eating well and on time, but for whom cooking every day has become a strain.

A household with a specific diet

Dietary preferences or a doctor-advised diet that you want cooked consistently to those instructions, day after day.

Not sure whether you need Home Cook Services or Maid Services?

Home Cook Services is a dedicated cook — fresh meals prepared daily to your taste, and nothing else. Maid Services is general household help across cleaning, utensils, laundry and chores. If cooking is really what you need, choose a home cook; if you want a bit of everything, Maid Services fits better.

What to expect

How Arranging Home Cook Services Works

Five stages, told from your side, so you know what you are agreeing to before you agree to it.

1 · First contact

Call or message and tell us the basics — where you are in Kota, how many people you cook for, and the meals and timings you have in mind.

2 · We listen first

We ask about your family's taste, any dietary preferences, and how many meals a day — then tell you what kind of arrangement fits.

3 · Matching the right person

We suggest verified cooks whose experience fits your kind of food and share their details. You decide who you are comfortable having in your kitchen.

4 · Scheduling and start

We agree the meals, timings and the routine, make expectations clear on both sides, and the cook starts.

5 · Ongoing support

We stay reachable while the service runs. If tastes change or the fit is not right, tell us and we will sort it out.

Transparency

How Pricing Works

We do not publish rates, because a number set before we know your household would not be an honest one. What we can do is tell you exactly what the charge depends on.

  • The scope of cooking — the number of meals and how many people you cook for.
  • Duration — days per week, and how long the arrangement is expected to run.
  • Meals and timings — how many meals a day and when you need them.
  • Specific requirements — the kind of food, dietary preferences, and anything particular to your household.

We discuss the exact charge with you once we understand what you need, and there is no obligation to go ahead after that conversation.

Common questions

Home Cook Services — Common Questions

The things households ask us on the phone, answered here so you do not have to.

What does a home cook from Quality Care Services do?

They prepare fresh, home-style meals for your household, cooked daily to your family's taste. That includes planning around the meals you want, cooking them fresh, and following any dietary preferences or instructions you give — vegetarian, low-oil, particular cuisines, or a diet a family member has been advised to follow. We agree the meals and timings with you before the cook starts.

Does the cook also clean or do household chores?

No — cooking is the job. A home cook prepares meals and keeps their own cooking area tidy as they work, but general household chores, laundry, dusting and routine cleaning are not part of the service. If you need that as well, it is Maid Services or Housekeeping, which we can arrange separately or alongside.

Can the cook follow our dietary preferences or restrictions?

Yes. Tell us your household's preferences — vegetarian or non-vegetarian, regional cuisines, less oil or spice, or a specific diet a family member follows — and we match a cook who can work to them. Where a diet has been advised by your own doctor, the cook follows the instructions you pass on; we do not give medical or nutritional advice ourselves.

How many meals a day, and can we set the timings?

Yes. We arrange cooking once, twice or through the day, at the times that suit your household — breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a specific combination. Tell us how many meals and when, and we will tell you honestly what fits.

Are your home cooks verified?

Yes. All staff go through a verification process before placement, including identity verification, background checks, and an assessment of their experience and suitability for the work. We share the relevant details with you before the service begins, because they will be cooking in your kitchen and for your family.

How are home cook charges decided?

Charges depend on the number of meals and people cooked for, how many days per week, the kind of food, and the timings you need. We discuss the exact charge with you after we understand what you need, with no obligation to proceed. We do not publish rates on this page because a number set without knowing your household would not be an honest one.
