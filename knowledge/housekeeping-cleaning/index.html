Knowledge · Housekeeping

Arranging Household Cleaning

"Clean" means something different in every household, which is why so many cleaning arrangements end in quiet disappointment on both sides. This section covers how to say what you actually expect, room by room, and how to judge the standard fairly once someone is doing the work.

What this section covers

The Practical Questions, Answered Plainly

These are the things families ask us, and the ones they only think to ask after an arrangement has disappointed them. Writing them down here does not replace that call — it just means you are not starting from nothing.

What we write about

  • Setting room-by-room expectations, so "thorough" means the same thing to everyone
  • How often is genuinely needed — daily, alternate days, or scheduled visits
  • Judging the standard fairly, and raising a shortfall without souring the arrangement
  • Cleaning in a home where someone is unwell, bedridden, or a newborn is present
  • Supplies and equipment — who provides what, and storing them safely

What we will not write about

  • Infection control or clinical disinfection protocols — practical household hygiene is not the same thing and we will not blur them
  • Health claims about particular cleaning products or methods
  • Fixed rates — what the work is worth depends on the home, the rooms and the frequency

Where a household has a patient at home, the treating medical team may set specific hygiene requirements. We will place someone who can follow those instructions carefully, but the instructions themselves must come from the doctor or nursing staff who know the case, not from us.

The service behind this section

What Housekeeping Means Here

Cleaning as the main job — thorough dusting, floors, surfaces, bathrooms and the kitchen area, room by room. Every person we place is verified and briefed on the agreed scope before the first visit.

Cleaning is the job, not an extra

This service exists because cleaning added on top of other duties is the first thing to get rushed. Here it is the whole placement, with the time to do it properly.

Thoroughness over speed

We weigh thoroughness more heavily than formal experience when placing for this service. It is the quality families actually notice.

Verified before placement

Identity, address and background checks are completed before anyone enters your home. The same standard applies to every placement without exception.
